Ava Home

SMS consent and enrollment

Ava Home is a private personal AI assistant for one local user. SMS is optional and disabled by default. This page documents local enrollment; it is not a public signup form and does not collect phone numbers.

How enrollment works

  1. The sole user enters their own mobile number through Ava Home’s local provisioning tool. The configuration is encrypted locally. Saving configuration alone does not grant SMS consent or enable sending.
  2. After configuration is saved, the tool displays the consent notice below.
  3. The user must enter I AGREE exactly at the consent prompt. Declining, entering other text, cancelling, or aborting leaves consent disabled.
  4. Ava Home records consent for the local recipient and SMS channel, with the notice version. The phone number is not copied into the consent record. Missing, revoked, malformed, or differently versioned consent blocks delivery. Other delivery permissions and controls must also allow sending.

Opting out and getting help

Reply STOP to revoke local SMS consent. Reply HELP for the application’s fixed help response. These commands bypass AI conversation processing. The application recognizes these two keywords without regard to letter case, after removing surrounding whitespace and validating the sender and incoming request.

Ava Home itself does not generate a STOP confirmation text. This page does not assert that a separate messaging-provider confirmation is configured. SMS keyword enrollment or re-enrollment is not documented here; initial consent is collected through the local process above.
