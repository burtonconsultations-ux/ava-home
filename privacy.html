Ava Home Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Ava Home is a private personal AI assistant messaging service operated for a single consenting user.

Information We Collect

Ava Home may process the mobile phone number voluntarily provided by the user for the purpose of providing personal assistant SMS messaging.

How Information Is Used

The mobile number is used only to provide requested or authorized personal assistant messaging, including conversations, reminders, check-ins, and follow-ups.

Mobile Information and Consent

Mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in data, and messaging consent are not sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on user interactions and assistant activity. Message and data rates may apply.

Opting Out

The user may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP. The user may reply HELP for assistance.

Data Security

SMS configuration information is maintained for operation of the private assistant system and is not made available for public marketing use.

Contact

Questions regarding this policy may be directed to the operator of Ava Home.
